# MAT-11 · TEACHING HANDOFF — Mathematics (041)
*What her Maths teacher needs from this account, from the Professors' own lists at the staff meeting of 2026-11-20. This account has no memory of her classes; this file is that memory.*

## The pack: the unit of delivery
- **One file per chapter:** `outputs/packs/MAT-PACK-CH<nn>.md`, stamped with its coverage ("based on 2026–2025"), rebuilt each time a year closes.
- **Each question carries:** year · set (paper code) · question number · marks · the question as printed · the marking scheme's own lines, word for word, each with its mark · its pattern (code and plain name).
- **The last section is Test-bank ready:** the same questions with their KEY lines, because her Sunday tests use only banked questions.
- **Few files:** her study projects break when a long list of files is added, so one chapter is one file and nothing else is needed to use it.
- **How it reaches her class:** the file lives in this repository on GitHub, which both her accounts can reach; she adds that one file to the class project, or downloads and attaches it.
- **When:** about 14 days before teaching reaches the chapter. Her teachers set the order; she names the chapter when she asks.
- **Never** invent a question to fill a gap; a thin pack says how thin, with the numbers.

## What Maths needs (Professor Maths)
- **Years:** 2026 back to 2022 first; she already owns an Oswaal chapter-wise book of board questions from before 2021.
- **Marking scheme:** every line word for word with its mark: the condition (x ≠ 0, the interval), the method line, the final statement.
- **Transcription:** from the page image only, in one linear notation; matrices row by row; piecewise endpoints exact (≤ or <); LPP and area graphs as vertices, inequalities and the shaded side.
- **Families that matter:** continuity at a point, maxima and minima, integration, area, LPP, case-based. Their counts come from the 2026-27 sample paper.
- **First wanted:** the Ch 5 Continuity and Differentiability pack, then the chapters in her teaching order.

## The rule this account enforces on the teachers
- Professors propose; only a printed page proves. A pattern a teacher suggests stays DRAFT until a real question verifies it.
